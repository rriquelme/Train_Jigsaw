# Privacy Policy — Jigsaw Trainer

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

Jigsaw Trainer ("the app") is a free, offline puzzle-training game for Android,
published by Roberto Riquelme.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.**
It requests no permissions and has no internet access.

## Data the app handles

- **Game progress and settings** (current and best streak, play statistics,
  background colour, practice mode, timer preference) are stored only on your
  device, in the app's private storage. The app never transmits them.
- **Photos you choose** through the "Your photo" option are read once from the
  Android photo picker and saved in the app's private storage so they can be
  reused in later sessions. They are never uploaded anywhere. You can remove them
  from inside the app (picker → Edit) or by clearing the app's storage or
  uninstalling the app.
- **Bundled sample images** are part of the app package.

## Device backup

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, your device may include the
app's game progress and settings in the backup of your device, which is handled
by your device's backup provider (normally your Google account) under that
provider's privacy policy. The app excludes your photos from this backup. You can
turn device backup off in Android Settings.

## Network access

The app declares no `INTERNET` permission. It cannot send or receive data over
the network.

## Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party
SDKs.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. It
is rated for a general audience.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all
data the app holds.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address
